Hormone therapy

Personalised hormone options.

Your specialist will recommend the form that best matches your symptoms, lifestyle, and medical history.

Most prescribed

Daily Tablet

The most commonly prescribed form of systemic HRT. Taken once daily, it delivers consistent hormone levels throughout the day.

Hot flashes Sleep Mood Joint pain

Skin Patch

Applied to the skin twice weekly, patches bypass the digestive system and deliver hormones directly into the bloodstream.

Hot flashes Sleep Brain fog

Daily Gel

A pump gel applied once daily to the skin. Offers dose flexibility and is well tolerated by women who prefer not to swallow tablets.

Hot flashes Energy Mood

Combined Single Tablet

One daily tablet containing both oestrogen and progesterone. Suitable for women who still have their uterus and prefer a single, convenient option.

Hot flashes Bone health Mood Libido
